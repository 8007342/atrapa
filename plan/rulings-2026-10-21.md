# Operator rulings — 2026-10-21

These rulings settle the open gameplay review (tasks 1.2/1.3) for v1, and they set how the work is split. They override conflicting text in `openspec/changes/grid-game-and-local-ai/`. The design review `review-2026-10-21.md` is accepted except where this file changes it.

## Engine
- One shared **pure-Dart rules engine**: `packages/atrapa_rules`, with no Flame or Flutter imports. The app, the scripted bots and the trainer all run this same engine. The purpose is runtime predictability: the AI plays the exact rules it was trained on.

## Players
- A match has 2–4 snakes: 1 human vs 1, 2 or 3 AIs.
- A demo mode allows 2 humans on one keyboard: WASD for one snake, arrow keys for the other.
- The trainer must therefore train with multiple AI snakes in one arena (2–4 snakes, self-play plus scripted opponents).

## Rules v1
- **Controls:** the arrow in your current heading boosts, the opposite arrow brakes, and a perpendicular arrow turns. At most one turn per cell boundary; the latest press wins.
- **Growth:** snakes start at a fixed length and **grow as the match progresses**. Cutting off and enclosing opponents is the core strategy. Body collision with any snake, including your own, loses.
- **Collisions:** hitting a wall or any body loses. When two heads meet in the same cell or swap places, both lose.
- **End of match:** one survivor wins, or the match ends at a tick limit.
- **Powerups:** v2. The first planned one is a *helmet*: while you wear it, a BOOSTING head-on collision is a win for the helmet wearer. More powerups will follow, so the observation and action format must be versioned and cheap to retrain.

## Scripted teacher and baseline
- The free-space bot: count free cells reachable within a Manhattan distance in the candidate directions and turn toward the most empty space. This is the operator's TurboC heuristic, and it was "reasonably hard to beat". It is the fallback opponent and the first imitation teacher.

## Work split
- **Forge (Codex), most of the project:** the Flame app, `/play`, keyboard and touch controls, the in-browser Dart evaluator, UX, the Cloudflare preview, and from v1 onwards the evolution of `packages/atrapa_rules` (new rules, powerups, schema versions).
- **macuahuitl (Claude), compute only:** it bootstrapped rules engine v1, the teacher and the trainer so training could start. From here it retrains and exports `assets/policies/*` whenever the engine or schema version changes. The laptops never train.
- **Integration:** branch `game`, with `work/<topic>` branches merged through PRs into `game`. A rules change bumps the schema version and updates the golden replay; that version bump is the signal to retrain.
